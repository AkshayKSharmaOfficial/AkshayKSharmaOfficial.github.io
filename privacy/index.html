Privacy Policy

What this website collects, why, and your choices.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

This website collects as little personal information as possible. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Information you send through the contact form

When you use the contact or collaboration form, you provide your name, email address, an optional organisation name, the type of enquiry and your message. This is used only to read and reply to your enquiry.

How the message is delivered

Your message is sent to the site owner's email inbox, using an email delivery service or a form-processing service configured by the owner. That service processes the message on the owner's behalf only to deliver it. The contact form does not keep a database of submissions.

Quiz and certificates

When you pass a quiz, the name and mobile number you entered, the quiz name, your score and the date are saved on the site's server so your certificate can be verified. Anyone who knows a Certificate ID can see the name, quiz, score and date for that certificate on the verification page. Your mobile number is never shown publicly and is not shared; only the site owner can see it. You can ask for your record to be deleted by messaging Akshay Kumar Sharma on LinkedIn.

Sensitive information

This website never asks for banking passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, card numbers or other sensitive financial information. Please do not send them.

Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies. Your browser may store your English or Hindi language choice locally on your device so that the choice is remembered. This is not used for tracking and is never sent to the server.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Form input is validated on the server and the form includes basic spam protection that does not rely on third-party tracking.

External links

This website links to external sites such as LinkedIn, YouTube and Government of India portals. Their privacy practices are their own.

Your choices

You can ask for your enquiry to be deleted from the owner's inbox at any time. To do so, message Akshay Kumar Sharma on LinkedIn (opens in a new tab).

Educational purpose

This website is an educational resource. It is not an official government reporting portal or an emergency response service.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest revision.
