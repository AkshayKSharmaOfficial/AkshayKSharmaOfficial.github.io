Explainable Phishing Email Detection: Showing Users Why an Email Looks Unsafe

Proposed Research IdeaPhishing Detection

Abstract

A proposed study on whether a phishing checker that explains its warning (for example a mismatched link or a false sense of urgency) helps ordinary users make better decisions than one that only shows a score.

Research objective

To explore which email features (sender address, link destination, urgency wording, attachment type) are the most useful to show users, and whether plain-language explanations make people more likely to stop and verify.

Methodology

Planned approach: build a small rule-based checker as an extension of the Phishing Email Detection project; use only publicly available or self-created sample emails; compare user decisions with and without explanations through a short anonymous classroom survey. No real user emails will be collected.

Conclusion

This is a proposed research idea. No data has been collected yet and there are no results or findings. It will be updated if and when the work is carried out.
