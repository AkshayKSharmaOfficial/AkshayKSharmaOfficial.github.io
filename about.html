Professional Introduction

Akshay Kumar Sharma is a Cyber Security Educator and Researcher focused on cybersecurity education, digital safety, and public cyber awareness. His mission is to make cybersecurity knowledge easier to understand and help individuals develop safer digital habits.

Through educational content, research-oriented learning, projects, and awareness initiatives, he aims to contribute to a more informed and secure digital society.
